Welcome to University of Technology

About Our University:
The University of Technology is a leading institution of higher education founded in 1950. We are committed to providing world-class education and fostering innovation in technology, engineering, and applied sciences.

Academic Programs:
- Computer Science and Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Data Science and Analytics
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Cybersecurity
- Software Engineering
- Information Technology

Admissions:
- Application deadline: March 15th for Fall semester
- Minimum GPA requirement: 3.0
- SAT score requirement: 1200 or higher
- Application fee: $75
- Required documents: Transcripts, letters of recommendation, personal statement

Tuition and Fees:
- Undergraduate tuition: $15,000 per semester
- Graduate tuition: $18,000 per semester
- Room and board: $8,000 per semester
- Technology fee: $500 per semester
- Student activity fee: $200 per semester

Financial Aid:

- Work-study programs
- Student loans through federal programs
- Payment plans available
