Welcome to Example College!

Example College is a mid-sized institution located in Springfield. We offer undergraduate and postgraduate programs across the School of Engineering, School of Arts & Humanities, School of Business, and School of Natural Sciences.

- Engineering: Computer Science, Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, and Data Science.
- Arts & Humanities: English, History, Philosophy, and Psychology.
- Business: Accounting, Finance, Marketing, and MBA.
- Natural Sciences: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Environmental Science.

Admissions:
- Application deadlines: Fall (June 1), Spring (November 15)
- Average SAT: 1250; Average GPA: 3.6
- Scholarships available based on merit and need

Campus:
- Library open 8am–11pm on weekdays
- Housing guaranteed for first-year students
- Career services offer internships and placement support
